BEFORE YOU BRAWL

SEED SURGES

Magic seeds pop out of the centre plaza. Walk over them (or press E) to bank them for your team.

Bank 12 and your team gets a SEED SURGE: faster running and faster attacks for 10 s. Most surges wins the round.

Getting hit makes your team drop seeds, so don't get greedy.

HEALTH

You have 8 hearts. There's no slow regen, so heal on purpose:

Every seed you pick up heals half a heart. Walk into a berry bush to heal 1 heart (it regrows in 20 s).

Knocked out? You're back in 4 s at full health. A Pumpkin Shield blocks most hits from the front.

ENERGY

The blue Energy bar pays for your weapon special (right-click) and hedges (Q, 20 each, up to 3).

It refills slowly on its own, faster when you land hits, and every seed gives +5.
