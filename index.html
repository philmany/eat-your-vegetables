BEFORE YOU BRAWL

SEED SURGES

Magic seeds pop out of the centre plaza. Walk over them (or press Eor tap them) to bank them for your team.

Bank 12 and your team gets a SEED SURGE: faster running and faster attacks for 10 s. Most surges wins the round.

Get knocked out and you drop 75% of the seeds you banked, so don't get greedy.

HEALTH

You have 8 hearts. There's no slow regen, so heal on purpose:

Every seed you pick up heals half a heart. Walk intoTap a berry bush (look for the red cross) to heal 2 hearts (it regrows in 20 s).

Knocked out? You're back in 4 s at full health. A Pumpkin Shield blocks most hits from the front.

ENERGY

The blue Energy bar pays for your weapon special (right-clickblue button) and hedges (QHEDGE button, 20 each, up to 3).

It refills slowly on its own, faster when you land hits, and every seed gives +5.

COMEBACKS

Falling behind (1 surge, or 3 knockouts in Team Deathmatch)? Your team gets a small COMEBACK boost:

Respawn in 3 s instead of 4. A Golden Seed pops up near your side (worth 3 seeds and heals 1 heart).

Their top scorer wears a BOUNTY: knock them out for +1 knockout and 3 bonus seeds.
